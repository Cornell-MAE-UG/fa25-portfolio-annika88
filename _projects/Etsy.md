  <h3 style="color: black;">Project Outline</h3>
  <p style="color: black;">

  Back during Covid, I decided to teach myself how to crochet so I could make myself a big blanket. I loved it so much I started to make little plushies to decorate my room with, and that evolved into selling my creations on Etsy! It started with the little dinosours, with the triceratops being my most popular item. I began to make more creatures and even do custom orders for people. The whole experience was very rewarding, especially receiving reviews and photos from customers thanking me! I ran this business completely on my own: after learning how to crochet, I learned how to start an Etsy website, edited my own photos, posted my own listings, made my own plushies, packed and shipped out the orders all by myself at the age of 13. Additionally, I donated a portion of my proceeds to Oceana, as an ocean lover myself. Right now I have my Etsy shop paused to focus on school, but I plan on reactivating it soon!
  </p>
